## Design Specificity Verdict

The screens the owner likes each have one game on them, so the game's art and colour supply the identity. The four tabs have no single game to borrow from, so what shows is the chassis underneath, and that chassis comes from three other apps:

- Home and Search are a music app's shelves: a big bold heading, a one-line subtitle, a rail, 32dp, repeat (SimpMusic, the owner's reference for controls and spacing).
- Profile is Instagram's header: face on the left, four counts, bio, a wide button, an icon strip.
- News is an RSS reader: a column of identical image cards.

Letterboxd, the acknowledged reference for structure and tone, contributes almost nothing to their structure. Any app could wear these four screens unchanged.

Cognitive load: 2 of 8 checklist items fail (visual hierarchy; minimal choices: seven scope pills on Search, five on News). Moderate.

Emotional journey: the peaks are the bespoke screens; sessions start and end on the tabs, which are the valleys. Nothing on Home or Profile marks a shelf growing after a log.

## Overall Impression

Well built and unauthored. Loading, errors and empty states are handled with care; the composition is one template applied four times. The biggest opportunity: keep SimpMusic for the controls and give the structure of these four screens to Letterboxd.

## What's Working

- The ground is already right: the page colour `#14171c` is within a shade of Letterboxd's slate, and the 13sp body and 11sp caption are in its range.
- Every band fails alone and says so; skeletons match the size of what replaces them.
- Honest empty states: a new account gets "Start your shelf" and one action, never fake activity.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The navigation does not hold the product's core loop.** Logging is a + in the top-left corner of the Profile tab. Friends' activity has no destination, only a bell on Home. Backlog and wishlist appear nowhere on Home or Profile. News, content GameLog does not own, holds a quarter of the bar. Fix: choose a bar map (below). Command: /impeccable shape.

**[P1] Home's first screen is a greeting, not games.** Wordmark, "Welcome back" block and the Surprise Me card put the first rail's covers about 430dp down; the first screen holds one row of three covers. Three news cards at about 326dp each make news roughly a third of the page. Fix: one-line greeting in the masthead, covers within the first 150dp, news as compact rows or off Home. Command: /impeccable layout.

**[P1] One template, repeated, with no hierarchy.** Every band is a 20sp bold heading, a subtitle, and the same 120×180 rail or the same card. Home and untyped Search are hard to tell apart. Fix: two cover sizes with meaning (large for the catalogue, small for people's activity), lists where content is ranked or dated, one quiet heading voice. Command: /impeccable layout, then /impeccable typeset.

**[P2] The profile hides what a logger's profile is for.** Counts are 14sp; the four sections are unlabeled icons; no score distribution; backlog and wishlist absent. Fix: favourites as the hero row, recent activity as covers with scores, a score histogram, a labelled list of counts. Command: /impeccable shape.

**[P2] Colour carries nothing on the tabs.** The only colour in the chrome is blue, mostly on five "See all" links, which read as web links. Score and status colours barely appear outside the review card. Fix: "See all" in quiet grey; blue for the primary action and selection only; a score chip and status glyph under covers wherever a person's activity is shown. Command: /impeccable colorize.

## Ways to structure it

The bar:
- A. Letterboxd's map: Home · Search · + · Activity · Profile; News becomes a fourth section inside Home (Letterboxd's Journal); Activity holds friends, you and notifications. Cost: a new Activity screen, five slots.
- B. Keep four, add the +: Home · Search · News · Profile with a round + on the bar; friends' activity becomes Home's first row.
- C. Leave the bar: only the bands are restyled.

Home:
- Friends first: sections across the top (Games · Reviews · Collections · News); first row "New from friends", small covers four across with the friend's face and score under each; then Popular this week, then Releases.
- Your shelf first: first row "Now playing", in-progress games as large covers with status; then friends; then popular.
- Tighten only: keep the four bands, one-line greeting, no subtitles, news as rows.

Search:
- Browse list: under the seven tabs, untyped Games becomes a plain list of doors (Release calendar, Genres, Most popular, Highest rated, Must Play); the rails move to Home.
- Keep Discover, change its rhythm: genres as one row of chips instead of a five-row grid (about 400dp before the first game today); "Most popular" as a numbered list of ten.

News:
- Fold it into Home as the fourth section (with bar option A).
- Make it a front page: one lead story, then compact rows; trailers as one rail on top; five sub-tabs become three.

Profile:
- Letterboxd's order: header, four favourites full width, "Recent activity" as four covers with scores, the score histogram, then a list with numbers on the right (Games, Reviews, Collections, Backlog, Wishlist, Physical, Following, Followers).
- Keep the Instagram header: label the four tabs, enlarge the counts, add the histogram above the tabs.

Sizes, type and colour:
- Catalogue covers stay 120×180 in rails.
- One small cover, 76×114, four across, for people's activity only (the size the Surprise Me deck and the profile's favourites already use).
- Headings: keep 20sp bold and drop the subtitles, or take Letterboxd's voice (11–12sp, grey, capitals, a hairline under, "More" at the right).
- Body 13sp and caption 11sp stay.

Cautions:
- A friends-first Home is empty for an account that follows nobody, which today is most accounts; each friends row needs a fallback.
- Some options reopen earlier owner decisions: covers three across, sentence-case labels, the menu removed from Search.
